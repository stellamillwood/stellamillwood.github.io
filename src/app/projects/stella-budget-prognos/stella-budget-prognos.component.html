Stakeholder Request → Reframed Need

The initiative originated from headquarters stakeholders who, at first, requested dashboards with data visualizations. Early discussions revealed the issue was not visualization alone. Users struggled with navigation, information structure, and finding key actions across complex budget types. This reframed the assignment from “add dashboards” to “research how users find and understand relevant data overviews and identify pain points”.

Exploratory Interviews and User Tests

One department expressed that their needs had been underrepresented in previous projects. In response, I partnered with an experienced business controller from the underrepresented department to define the user sample and co-create a combined interview and user test script focused on navigation and data understanding.

  • 10 users
  • 60-minute individual sessions
  • 3 user roles: business controllers, operations coordinators, managers
  • 3 departments represented across different mission areas

Analysis Through Affinity Mapping

The insights were continuously validated with a business controller to ensure domain accuracy and reduce interpretation bias.

Affinity map of tagged data.

Key Findings

  • Unclear folder names and hierarchies make it difficult to find relevant data.
  • Users, at times, depend on their memory to navigate the application.
  • Difficult to compare data between reports to gain an overview.
    • Pen, paper and calculator are sometimes used.
  • Lack of visual cues and guidance make it difficult to contextualize data, identify outliers, and perform actions with intent and understanding.
    • Trial and error, even with experienced users.
    • Manual work to recalulate and trace the data in order to understand it and put it into context.
  • Heavy reliance on Excel
  • Inconsistent ways to interpret the same data across systems make it difficult to understand the data.

Problem Statements

Based on findings, I developed concise, actionable problem statements. I presented them to the stakeholders, and now a prioritization is needed in order to narrow the scope.
